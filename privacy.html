Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The Daily Record is a personal app used by its developer. This policy explains how it handles data.

Google Drive access

With your permission, the app accesses your Google Drive only to back up and sync its own files — your journal, conversation history, and app settings — in a TheDailyRecord folder in your own Drive, and to keep older versions of your journal from being deleted. It does not read, change, or share any other files.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on your own devices and in your own Google Drive. The app has no server of its own and does not collect, sell, or share your data with anyone.

AI features

When you use an AI feature (for example, a chat), the text involved is sent to the AI provider you configured (such as Anthropic) using your own API key, solely to produce a response.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access to Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/cbarru1/the-daily-record-app.
